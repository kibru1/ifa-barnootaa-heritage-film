# ETHICS AND RESPONSIBLE AI USE
## Ifa Barnootaa

### 1. AI Disclosure
The film includes AI-generated visual assets, AI-assisted
Afaan Oromo narration, and original synthesized background
music. An AI-generated content disclosure end card appears
at the end of the film.

### 2. Fictional Character
The main student is fictional. The project does not intend
to portray a real identifiable person.

### 3. Voice and Identity
The narration uses an AI text-to-speech model. No real
person's voice was cloned or imitated.

### 4. Cultural Representation
The film celebrates education in an Ethiopian setting.
The visuals are illustrative and should not be interpreted
as documentary evidence about a particular community.

### 5. Model Attribution and Licensing
The Afaan Oromo TTS model used is:
facebook/mms-tts-orm

Its model license is CC-BY-NC 4.0. Attribution should be
provided, and commercial use must respect the non-commercial
license conditions.

The image-generation model used is:
stabilityai/sd-turbo

Users should review the applicable model license and terms
before redistribution or commercial use.

### 6. Limitations and Transparency
The film uses animated still images rather than full
AI-generated video motion. Its background score is
synthesized and is not a traditional field recording.

### 7. Human Review
The creator should review Afaan Oromo narration and English
subtitles for linguistic accuracy. Audience feedback should
be collected and documented honestly.
