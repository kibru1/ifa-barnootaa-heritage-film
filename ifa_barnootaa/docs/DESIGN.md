# DESIGN DOCUMENT
## Ifa Barnootaa – The Light of Education

### 1. Project Intent
The project is an AI-assisted Afaan Oromo heritage short film
celebrating the importance of education in individual and
community development.

### 2. Intended Audience
Afaan Oromo speakers, students, educators, families, and
communities interested in the value of education.

### 3. Story and Creative Direction
The film follows a fictional Ethiopian student whose commitment
to learning is supported by teachers, family, and community.
The story communicates hope, perseverance, and the transformative
power of education.

### 5. Hardest Production Challenge
Maintaining a consistent visual identity across ten scenes
while producing the film with limited computing resources.

### 6. Solution
A character reference description and a consistent visual
prompt were used when generating the keyframes. The still
images were animated using FFmpeg pan-and-zoom effects.

### 7. Creative and Technical Decisions
The film uses AI-assisted Afaan Oromo narration, AI-generated
visuals, original synthesized background music, and English
subtitles. A vertical and horizontal version were exported.

### 8. Limitations
The video scenes use animated still images rather than
full AI-generated character motion. The background music
is synthesized and is not a recording of traditional
Ethiopian instruments.
