## About the Project

**Ifa Barnootaa (The Light of Education)** is an AI-generated heritage short film that celebrates the transformative power of education in Ethiopian communities.

The film tells the story of a young student's educational journey, highlighting the importance of teachers, family support, determination, knowledge, and equal educational opportunities.

This project combines artificial intelligence, storytelling, visual generation, narration, and original music to create a short cinematic experience in Afaan Oromo.

## Project Objectives

- Celebrate the importance of education in society.
- Promote Afaan Oromo through AI-assisted digital storytelling.
- Explore AI-powered film and multimedia generation.
- Demonstrate how AI technologies can support cultural storytelling.
- Create an engaging short film with narration and original background music.

## Production Workflow

1. Story development and script generation.
2. Storyboard creation and validation.
3. Character and visual style development.
4. AI-generated keyframes and video shots.
5. Afaan Oromo narration generation or recording.
6. Original background music generation.
7. Video editing, subtitles, and audio mixing.
8. Exporting vertical and horizontal film versions.
9. Audience testing and project documentation.

## Final Deliverables

The project aims to provide:

- A 60–90 second Afaan Oromo short film.
- Vertical 9:16 MP4 version.
- Horizontal 16:9 MP4 version.
- Narration and original background music.
- English subtitles.
- A project walkthrough video.
- Technical documentation and experiment logs.

*Production is in progress. Final outputs will be added as they are completed.*

## Ethical Considerations

- AI-generated content will be identified transparently.
- Original music and visuals will be used responsibly.
- Real people's faces and voices will not be used without permission.
- Cultural representation will be reviewed carefully.
- Audience feedback will inform improvements.
